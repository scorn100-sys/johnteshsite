Why John Tesh

Three decades of music built for picture.

A catalog shaped for music supervisors, not just listeners.

For more than three decades, John Tesh has created music that blends cinematic orchestration, contemporary instrumentation, emotional piano performance, inspirational themes, sports energy, holiday warmth, and broadcast ready production. His catalog spans uplifting instrumentals, dramatic orchestral works, romantic melodies, faith based compositions, seasonal favorites, and high energy sports music, offering music supervisors a versatile collection suitable for film, television, advertising, trailers, documentaries, sports programming, branded content, and digital media.

Whether a project calls for inspiration, triumph, reflection, intimacy, excitement, or celebration, the John Tesh catalog provides emotionally resonant music that enhances storytelling while remaining instantly accessible to broad audiences.

Credentials
  • Multi platinum recording artist
  • Emmy winning composer
  • Creator of the iconic "Roundball Rock" theme
  • Extensive catalog spanning orchestral, piano, pop instrumental, sports, holiday, inspirational, and cinematic music
  • Decades of broadcast, television, and live performance experience
